Meet the Founder

Wendell Killette

I didn't want to be a Scrum Master. In 2005, I was a QA engineer at Verizon when I was asked to step into the role, and I pushed back. Then I learned what Agile could do for teams, and I became a believer. I had always admired servant leaders, and once I saw that the role was built on serving the team, I knew it was the ideal role for me.

Twenty-plus years later, I've coached and mentored teams at Charles Schwab, USAA, LPL Financial, AT&T, American Airlines, Cigna, and the IRS. Before that, I earned a BS in Electronic Engineering, worked in field engineering and quality, and ran my own CCTV installation business. The thread through all of it: attention to detail, and the belief that the people behind the work matter most.

I started The Body of Work Studio because helping people grow is what drives me. Your career is a story worth telling well. Let's tell yours.
